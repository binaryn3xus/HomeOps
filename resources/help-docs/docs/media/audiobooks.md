# 🎧 Audiobooks

UNSC Fleet hosts a dedicated **Audiobookshelf** server for streaming audiobooks and podcasts with chapter navigation, variable playback speeds, sleep timers, and cross-device listening progress sync.

---

## 📚 Listening Options

### 🌐 Web Player
Listen directly in your browser without installing anything:
- **Web App:** [https://audiobooks.unscfleet.com](https://audiobooks.unscfleet.com)

---

## 📱 Mobile Apps

Enjoy seamless offline downloads, Apple CarPlay, and Android Auto by downloading a client app for your device.

=== "Android"

    The official **Audiobookshelf** client is available for all Android phones, tablets, and Android Auto head units.

    - [**Google Play Store: Audiobookshelf**](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.audiobookshelf.app){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

    #### Features
    - Native Android Auto support
    - Background playback with lock screen media controls
    - Offline downloading for trips and flights
    - Automatic sleep timers and bookmarks

=== "Apple (iOS / iPadOS)"

    Because the server is open-source, iOS users have great options to choose from on the App Store:

    - [**Apple App Store: Absorb for Audiobookshelf**](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/absorb-for-audiobookshelf/id6760673498){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

    #### Highlights
    - **Apple CarPlay Support:** Safely browse and listen to your library in your car.
    - **Offline Playback:** Download books with custom download quality directly to your phone.
    - **Progress Sync:** Matches your place on the web player automatically.

    > [!TIP] Progressive Web App (PWA)
    > You can also open [https://audiobooks.unscfleet.com](https://audiobooks.unscfleet.com) in Safari on iOS, tap **Share** (box with arrow), and choose **Add to Home Screen** to install the official Audiobookshelf web app with full offline support.

---

## 🔑 How to Connect Your App

1. Open the **Audiobookshelf** (Android) or **Absorb** (iOS) app.
2. In the **Server Address** field, enter:
   ```text
   https://audiobooks.unscfleet.com
   ```
3. Tap the **Sign in with Google** button to authenticate with your Google account.
4. Once authenticated, your personal audiobook library will load and start streaming!

> [!IMPORTANT] Google Account Authorization Required
> Before logging in for the first time, send your Google email address to an **Admin** so they can add your account to the approved Google users list.

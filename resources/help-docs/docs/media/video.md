# 🎬 Video Streaming

UNSC Fleet hosts dedicated high-definition movie and television libraries with 4K HDR support, automatic subtitle matching, and multi-device sync. You can access the library through **Plex** or **Jellyfin**.

---

## 📺 Streaming Platforms

=== "Plex"

    **Plex** offers a polished interface available natively on virtually every smart TV, streaming stick, game console, and mobile device.

    ### 🌐 Web Access
    Stream directly in your web browser:
    - **URL:** [https://plex.unscfleet.com](https://plex.unscfleet.com) (or [app.plex.tv](https://app.plex.tv))

    ### 📱 Download Apps
    Download the official Plex app on your devices:

    - [**Apple App Store (iOS / iPadOS / Apple TV)**](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/plex-find-movies-and-tv-shows/id383457673){ .md-button .md-button--primary }
    - [**Google Play Store (Android / Google TV)**](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.plexapp.android){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

    #### 📺 Smart TVs & Streaming Devices
    Search for **Plex** in your device's app store:
    - **Apple TV / Roku / Fire TV / Google TV / Android TV**
    - **Samsung Smart TVs (Tizen)** & **LG Smart TVs (webOS)**
    - **PlayStation & Xbox consoles**

    ### 🔑 Getting Access
    1. Create a free account at [plex.tv](https://www.plex.tv/) if you don't already have one.
    2. Send your Plex username or email to an **Admin**.
    3. Accept the server library invitation in your email or inside the Plex app under your profile!

=== "Jellyfin"

    **Jellyfin** is our 100% free, privacy-focused open-source media system with no subscriptions or tracking.

    ### 🌐 Web Access
    Stream directly from any browser:
    - **URL:** [https://jellyfin.unscfleet.com](https://jellyfin.unscfleet.com)

    ### 📱 Download Apps
    Download recommended clients for your devices:

    - [**Apple App Store: Swiftfin (iOS / iPadOS / Apple TV)**](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/swiftfin/id1604098728){ .md-button .md-button--primary }
    - [**Google Play Store: Jellyfin Mobile (Android)**](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.jellyfin.mobile){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

    #### 📺 Smart TVs & Streaming Devices
    - **Apple TV:** Install **Swiftfin** from the tvOS App Store.
    - **Google TV / Android TV / Fire TV:** Search for **Jellyfin** in the Google Play Store or Amazon App Store.
    - **Roku:** Install the official **Jellyfin** channel from the Roku Channel Store.

    ### 🔑 Setup Instructions
    1. Open the Jellyfin or Swiftfin app.
    2. When prompted for **Server Address**, enter:
       ```text
       https://jellyfin.unscfleet.com
       ```
    3. Enter the username and password provided by an **Admin**.

## 💡 Quick Tips

> [!TIP] Subtitles & Audio Tracks
> Both Plex and Jellyfin allow you to switch audio tracks (e.g. Director's Commentary, surround sound) and enable subtitles on the fly. If you prefer subtitles on by default, enable them in your app's account audio/subtitle preferences.

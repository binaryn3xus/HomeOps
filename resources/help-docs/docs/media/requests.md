# 🎯 Requesting Movies & TV Shows

Want a movie, anime, or new TV series added to our library? UNSC Fleet uses an automated request portal (**Seerr**) that lets friends and family search for content and request additions in seconds.

---

## 🌐 Access the Request Portal

- **URL:** [https://requests.unscfleet.com](https://requests.unscfleet.com)
  *(Also available at [https://seerr.unscfleet.com](https://seerr.unscfleet.com))*

---

## 🔑 How to Log In

1. Open [https://requests.unscfleet.com](https://requests.unscfleet.com).
2. Click **Sign In with Plex** (if you have a Plex account linked to our library), or enter your local credentials provided by an **Admin**.

---

## 🔍 How to Make a Request

1. **Search:** Use the search bar at the top or explore trending titles, upcoming releases, and popular genres.
2. **Select:** Click on any movie or TV series to view trailers, release dates, reviews, and current library availability.
3. **Request:**
   - **For Movies:** Click the green **Request** button.
   - **For TV Shows:** _Disabled for most users_
4. **Automated Processing:**
   - Once submitted, our media automation pipeline checks quality criteria and downloads the release automatically.
   - The media is indexed into **Plex** and **Jellyfin** as soon as the download finishes!

---

> [!NOTE] Notifications
> If you have your email linked or notifications configured, you'll receive an automated notification as soon as your requested title is ready to watch.
